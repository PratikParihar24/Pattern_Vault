Game Guide

How to Play

Everything you need to know to start playing, scoring, and climbing the global leaderboard.

Step by Step

Your path to victory

1

Create Your Account

Head to the Register page and enter your email, a secure password, and your display name. Your display name is what shows up on the global leaderboard — choose something memorable!

2

Login & Enter the Game

After logging in, you'll be presented with the quiz game. Click "Start Game" to begin your round of 5 questions from our shuffled question bank.

3

Answer the Questions

Each round has 5 questions with 4 options each (A, B, C, D). Select the answer you think is correct. You get instant visual feedback — green for correct, red for wrong.

4

Score & Submit

Each correct answer earns you 10 points. After 5 questions, your score is calculated and automatically submitted to the global leaderboard if it's your personal best.

5

Replay & Rise

Click "Play Again" to start a new round with freshly shuffled questions. Keep playing to beat your high score and climb the global leaderboard rankings.

Tips for Top Scores

🎯

Focus on Categories You Know

Questions span multiple domains. Lean into your strengths — tech, science, geography. Each has equal weight.

🔄

Replay Often

Questions are shuffled each round. The more you play, the more questions you'll see — and remember for next time.

⚡

Speed Isn't Everything

There's no timer penalty. Take your time to answer correctly. Accuracy is what pushes your score up.

The Rules

Fair play guidelines

1

One account per person. Creating multiple accounts to farm the leaderboard is against the spirit of fair competition.

2

Each round is 5 questions. You must answer all 5 before your score is submitted. Closing the tab mid-game forfeits that round.

3

Scores are cumulative highs. Only your best single-round score is shown on the leaderboard — not your total across all games.

4

Display names must be appropriate. Offensive or impersonating display names will be removed without notice.
