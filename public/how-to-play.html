Vault Access

How to open the Pattern Vault

1

Sign In

Create an account or log in to your MindMaster account. The Pattern Vault is available only during an authenticated session.

2

Open the Quiz

Go to the game screen. You can use the shortcut from the welcome screen or while a quiz round is open.

3

Trigger Vault Access

Mobile & All Devices: Follow your email's secret 5-question QWERTY pattern during the quiz round (each email maps to a unique option sequence A, B, C, or D per question).

Desktop / Developers: You can also press Ctrl + Shift + X anytime during the quiz to open the Pattern Vault instantly.

Tips for Top Scores

🎯

Focus on Categories You Know

Questions span multiple domains. Lean into your strengths — tech, science, geography. Each has equal weight.

🔄

Replay Often

Questions are shuffled each round. The more you play, the more questions you'll see — and remember for next time.

⚡

Speed Isn't Everything

There's no timer penalty. Take your time to answer correctly. Accuracy is what pushes your score up.
